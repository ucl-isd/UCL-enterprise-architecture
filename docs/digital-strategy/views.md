# Digital Strategy Views

Views describe how the strategy data is surfaced for different audiences —
dashboards, roadmaps, and reports generated from {term}`Essential (EAS)`.

## Digital Strategy view group

The portal landing page that groups all Digital Strategy views in Essential.

**Purpose:** Entry point to the full set of Digital Strategy views.
**Link:** [Digital Strategy view group](https://ucl.essentialintelligence.com/viewer/8b3b72ca41670a212b09_1/report?XML=8b3b72ca41670a212b09_1/reportXML.xml&PMA=store_18_Class10016&XSL=common/portal_template.xsl&cl=en-gb)

## Strategic Roadmap Dashboard

**Purpose:** Explore the full strategic plan data — objectives, initiatives, and
their scheduling over the 2026–32 horizon.
**Link:** [Strategic Roadmap Dashboard](https://ucl.essentialintelligence.com/viewer/8b3b72ca41670a212b09_1/report?XML=reportXML.xml&XSL=user/strategic_roadmap.xsl&LABEL=Strategic%20Roadmap%20Dashboard&cl=en-gb)

## Strategic Plans Cost Explorer

**Purpose:** Explore the costs associated with strategic plans, broken down by
per-year cost components (see the {doc}`data-model` for the `Cost` class).
**Link:** [Strategic Plans Cost Explorer](https://ucl.essentialintelligence.com/viewer/8b3b72ca41670a212b09_1/report?XML=8b3b72ca41670a212b09_1/reportXML.xml&XSL=user/strategic_plans_cost_explorer.xsl)

## Strategic Plan Completeness Report

**Purpose:** A data-quality report used during data population to check the
completeness of strategic plan records.
**Link:** [Strategic Plan Completeness Report](https://ucl.essentialintelligence.com/viewer/8b3b72ca41670a212b09_1/report?XML=reportXML.xml&XSL=user/strategic_plan_completeness_report.xsl)
