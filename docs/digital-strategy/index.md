## About the Digital Strategic Plan

The {term}`Digital Strategic Plan` sets out UCL's digital ambitions and
objectives over a seven-year horizon (2026–32). It provides oversight of future
investment needs, helps prioritise competing activity, and promotes guiding
Digital Principles for consistent decision-making across the university.

These elements are documented in {term}`Essential (EAS)`, UCL's enterprise
architecture tool, using its standard object types mapped to UCL-specific
terminology. The {doc}`metamodel` presents the conceptual UCL view, while the
{doc}`data-model` documents the concrete EAS classes and slots — including how
plans link to **costs** and **risks**.
